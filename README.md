# Miro's Workshop · 弥洛工坊

Miro's Workshop is an AI-assisted creative workshop for Obsidian templates and small tools for creative work. Miro Veylan is the workshop persona. The human account owner runs the workshop, reviews its work, and decides what to publish.

弥洛工坊是一间由 AI 辅助创作的小工坊，制作 Obsidian 模板和创作小工具。Miro Veylan 是工坊的创作角色。工坊由真人账号持有人运营，作品由账号持有人审核并决定发布。
